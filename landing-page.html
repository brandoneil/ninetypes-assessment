The Enneagram

More than a
personality label

The Enneagram is a practical tool for understanding why you do what you do — what motivates you, what gets in your way, and what kind of growth actually feels meaningful for you.

Through this assessment, you'll begin to identify the core patterns influencing how you respond to stress, relate to others, make decisions, pursue goals, and care for yourself.

01
Understand your motivation
Not just what you do, but why. The Enneagram goes beneath behavior to the underlying drive.
02
Recognise your patterns
See the recurring themes in how you respond to stress, conflict, and connection — with compassion, not judgment.
03
Improve your relationships
Understanding yourself is the first step to understanding others — and communicating with more care and clarity.
04
Find your growth edge
The Enneagram points to the specific growth practices most relevant to your type — not generic advice, but what actually moves the needle for you.
